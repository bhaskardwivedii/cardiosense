      In the full application

      This page is the prediction core. The deployed app (FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL) adds the clinic workflow around it.

      Accounts and roles

      Clinician and admin sign-in with secure tokens; admins manage who has access.

      Patient history

      Every assessment is stored, searchable by patient reference and filterable by risk level.

      PDF risk reports

      A one-page report with measurements, risk scale, explanation and follow-up for the patient file.

      All seven models live

      Score with any model or compare all seven for one patient; batch uploads of up to 10,000 rows.

      CardioSense is a decision-support aid for qualified clinicians, not a diagnostic device. Estimates come from a model trained on 68,576 records from the Kaggle Cardiovascular Disease dataset and are correct about 73% of the time on unseen patients.
